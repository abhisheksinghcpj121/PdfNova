Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about PDFNova? We've got answers.

Is PDFNova completely free to use? ▼
Yes! PDFNova offers free access to all core PDF operations including merging, splitting, compressing, rotating, editing, converting, and password protecting documents.
Are my uploaded files safe and private? ▼
Absolutely. Every upload is stored in a cryptographically isolated temporary folder protected with random capability tokens. Your files are automatically purged after 60 minutes, or you can delete them immediately with the "Delete File Now" button.
What file formats does PDFNova support? ▼
PDFNova natively supports PDF documents, images (JPG, PNG, WebP), Microsoft Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), and PowerPoint (.pptx).
What is the maximum file size limit? ▼
You can process documents up to 50 MB per file, and batches of up to 20 files at once.
Do you store or train AI models on my documents? ▼
No. PDFNova does not train AI models on user data and does not store or read document contents. Files exist only in temporary volatile storage during processing.
