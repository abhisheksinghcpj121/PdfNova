Data Safety & Confidentiality

Privacy & Security

Your privacy is important to us. Here's how we protect your documents and data.

Files are temporary

Automatically deleted from the server 60 minutes after processing.

No file storage

We do not store your documents permanently or index contents.

Secure processing

All files are processed in isolated environments with random UUIDs.

Your data stays private

We do not access, inspect, sell, or share your files with any third party.

1. Cryptographic Job Isolation & Capability Tokens

Every upload is assigned a cryptographically random UUID v4 and isolated in a separate subdirectory. Downloads and immediate deletions are strictly gated by high-entropy capability tokens (hex-encoded 24-byte random values). An attacker guessing a job ID cannot access the document without the corresponding secret token.

2. Automatic Expiry & Background Scheduled Cleanup

PDFNova runs an automatic garbage collection worker on a periodic schedule (every 15 minutes) as well as upon server boot. Once a job reaches its Time-To-Live (default 60 minutes), the entire directory containing input and output files is permanently deleted from the disk. Users can also trigger immediate deletion using the "Delete File Now" button.

3. Transparent Platform Considerations

PDFNova uses local ephemeral filesystem storage. Please note that on certain serverless platforms (e.g. AWS Lambda, Vercel), local filesystems are ephemeral and bound to container life cycles. We do not claim absolute data destruction beyond the filesystem scope, but guarantee our software never creates permanent database records or persistent cloud copies.
