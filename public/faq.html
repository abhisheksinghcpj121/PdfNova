Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PDFNova's features, privacy, and file conversions.

Is PDFNova completely free to use? ▼
Yes! PDFNova is completely free. There are no paywalls, hidden fees, watermarks added to your documents without your request, or mandatory sign-up screens.
Are my uploaded files safe and private? ▼
Yes. Every upload is placed in an isolated temporary folder named with a random UUID. Access to download or delete files requires a high-entropy secret token. All files are automatically removed after 60 minutes or immediately when you click "Delete File Now".
What file formats does PDFNova support? ▼
We support PDF documents (.pdf), images (JPG, PNG, WebP), and Microsoft Office files (.docx, .xlsx, .pptx). PDF-to-Word conversions run natively in pure JavaScript without external dependencies.
What is the maximum file size limit? ▼
The standard limit is 50 MB per file, and batches of up to 20 files can be uploaded simultaneously (for merging or batch conversions).
Do you store my files on the server? ▼
No permanent storage or database is used. Files exist only in volatile temporary directories during processing and are wiped clean by our scheduled garbage collection process.
How does the OCR (Optical Character Recognition) feature work? ▼
PDFNova includes a built-in OCR engine powered by Tesseract.js. It renders scanned document pages, detects text lines, and outputs both a plain text transcription and a searchable PDF with confidence markers.
What are the system requirements for Office to PDF conversion? ▼
To convert Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), or PowerPoint (.pptx) documents directly into PDF, LibreOffice headless must be installed on the host machine. If LibreOffice is not detected, PDFNova clearly displays an explanation and links to setup instructions. Note that PDF to Word (.docx) works out of the box without LibreOffice!
